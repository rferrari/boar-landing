Roadmap

First the roots. Then what grows.

The app comes first: make the everyday BOAR excellent, and let the people who carry it tell us where it falls short. Then it grows outward, into packs of knowledge that people make, share and take with them.

A direction, not a schedule. It will change as people use the app.

  1. Phase 1 · Here today

    Roots

    The part that already works, in v1.0.0 for Android. All of it runs on the phone, with no account and no network after the first setup, and it is measured on a real device.

    • Shipped

      Local offline engine

      Ask with no signal and BOAR finds the right passages in its library, by keyword and by meaning, then a model on your phone writes the answer.

      How it’s built

    • Shipped

      Your own knowledge base

      Bring your own notes, manuals and data (.txt, .md, .csv, .json or .pdf) and BOAR learns them on the phone, in collections you switch on and off.

      How importing works

    • Shipped

      Model browser

      Pick another model from the curated list, or search Hugging Face for a GGUF model and download it without leaving the app.

      Finding more models

    • Shipped

      Portable collections

      Export a collection as a plain JSON pack and hand it to someone; their phone rebuilds it locally, so it works on any device.

      About knowledge packs

  2. Phase 2 · Next

    First shoots

    Making the app itself better: on more phones, for more ways of asking, and ready before you leave home.

    • In progress

      iOS

      The same offline pipeline on iPhone, so BOAR goes with you whatever phone you carry.

    • Next

      Offline voice

      Speak your question and hear the answer read back, with speech recognition and a voice that both run on the phone, for when your hands are busy.

    • Next

      Packs for places

      Before a trip, download a ready-made pack for where you’re going: where to eat, how to get around, and maps, there even when your data plan isn’t.

  3. Phase 3 · After that

    Branches

    Once the app is solid, packs become something people make, share and trust, and what you keep on the phone stays yours.

    • Later

      A shared pack library

      Browse, preview and download knowledge packs curated by the community, from a directory built to work offline first.

    • Later

      Private memory

      Choose to encrypt the documents you import and your conversation history, so only you can read what’s stored on your phone.

    • Later

      The right model for each task

      Switch models per task or per question, a quick one for simple lookups and a bigger one for harder thinking, without restarting the conversation.

  4. Phase 4 · Further out

    Grove

    Where it could grow: knowledge worth keeping close, more places to build it, and ways to pass it on without the internet at all.

    • Further out

      Essentials library

      Verified offline bundles for first aid, basic survival, disaster response and growing your own food, checked before they reach your phone.

    • Further out

      Desktop workbench

      A companion for Mac and desktop, for people who build and look after large libraries and then carry them on the phone.

    • Exploring

      Sharing nearby

      Pass a pack from one BOAR phone to another over Bluetooth or local Wi-Fi, with no internet in between.

Help shape what grows next.

Tell us what you’d want in your pocket when the signal drops. Ideas, pack requests and bug reports all go in GitHub issues, and the code is MIT, open to anyone who wants to build.
