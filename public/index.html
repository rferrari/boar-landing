How it works

Install it, download once, go somewhere without signal.

  1. 01

    Install the APK

    Get boar-v1.0.0-arm64.apk from the release and check it against the published SHA-256. make setup can do the download, checksum and USB install for you.

  2. 02

    Download once

    On first launch BOAR fetches its default model, Qwen2.5-1.5B, plus a small embedding model: about 1 GB. That's the only time it needs the network.

  3. 03

    Switch on airplane mode

    Ask. BOAR searches its offline library (keyword + on-device embeddings), answers with the local model and shows the sources it used.

  • Sources on every answer. Each answer lists the offline articles it was built from.
  • A bigger library, optional. The Wikipedia Vital Articles pack: 49,832 articles in 164 MB.
  • Bring your own. Import your .txt, .md, .csv, .json and .pdf files, or search Hugging Face for another GGUF model.
  • Open. MIT licensed. Code, models, data sources and benchmark results are in the repo.

Not the dream, and we say so

Useful now beats perfect later.

The model everyone wants on a phone, a huge mixture-of-experts model streaming from storage, doesn't exist yet. BOAR is what works today, measured honestly, with a place ready for that model when it arrives.

4–15 tok/s is enough to find out how to treat a blister, what a museum is about, or why the train isn't running, when there's no signal and nobody around to ask.

  • Not measured yet: a comparison against internet search plus frontier models.
  • Not run yet: GrapheneOS on real hardware. The release build has no Google Play Services dependency.
  • Known weak spots: synthesis on the 1.5B model, and citation formatting.
  • poidh bounty #31: BOAR is claim #124 on "Build the Best Offline AI Research App for Android". The bounty is still open.

Keep your phone charged, pack BOAR, and go somewhere without signal.
