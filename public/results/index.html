About the score

Score v1, from 0 to 100: 60% speed (median tokens per second on completed answers of 16 tokens or more, full marks at 20), 25% answers completed and 15% expected sources found. A model that completes fewer than half its answers scores 0. It measures speed and reliability, not whether answers are correct.

The server computes every score from the answers themselves; the app can't send its own. Shared runs carry the phone's model, chipset, cores and CPU features, RAM and app version, never chats, documents, names or location. How the score works.
